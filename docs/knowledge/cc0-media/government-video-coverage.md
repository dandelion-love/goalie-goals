# Summary

Of the 9 cards, 13 goalkeeper moves and 4 progression families (26 rows), 13 have a
government clip whose publisher page was read and labels it public domain. Six more
have a lead seen only in search results (five distinct clips), and 7 have none found. Most of the clips come
from one source: a 2017 to 2020 series of short single-exercise demos by the Marine
Corps Training and Education Command and Marine Corps Base Quantico, on DVIDS. They are
20 to 60 seconds long, filmed by a Marine videographer, with a written description of
the start position, the movement and common errors. The goalkeeping technique moves
have no government footage: no ready position, dive, catch or punch. The app's own
figure has to be posed by hand from coaching sources for those, as the figure search
already concluded.[^gov-coverage]

# How to read the tables

- **Clip times** are minutes and seconds into the clip. Most clips are one demo from
  start to end, so the whole clip is given; no clip was watched in this pass, so a
  narrower window is set when a person first watches it.
- **Angle wanted** is the view the joint angles need: side for hip, knee and ankle
  flexion and trunk lean; front for knee tracking, lateral lean and foot width. The
  angle the clip actually shows was not seen, because no video was played. A person
  confirms it by eye before a clip goes to the pipeline.
- **Class**: PD is a US federal work labelled public domain on its publisher page. BY-SA
  would be an ESA item labelled CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO. Lead means the page was seen only in
  search results; its credit and label are read before use.
- Equipment in a clip (dumbbells, an ammo can, a suspension trainer, a sled, a box) is
  ignored; only body positions are measured. Every rule on the
  [concept page](/cc0-media/government-video.md) applies.[^gov-video]
