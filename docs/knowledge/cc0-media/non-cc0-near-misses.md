# Summary

These are open but not CC0. The app may use them under the credit rules of the media
Decision, but this search prefers CC0 so that rewards and path art carry no attribution
or share-alike duty.[^media-decision]

| Set | Licence | Duty | Use here |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenMoji | CC BY-SA 4.0[^icon-sets] | Credit; adapted glyphs stay CC BY-SA | Already chosen by the media Decision for playful glyphs; kept as the fallback if a CC0 redraw is not ready |
| Twemoji | CC BY 4.0[^icon-sets] | Credit | Not needed |
| game-icons.net | CC BY 3.0, per author[^game-icons-net] | Credit each author | Not needed; its fantasy style also clashes |
| Tabler, Lucide, Phosphor | MIT or ISC[^icon-sets] | Keep the notice | Interface icons only (settings, back, close), not reward art |
| OGA Medals | CC-BY 3.0[^oga-medals-site] | Credit | Excluded |
