# Summary

Free health bodies publish many exercise drawings, especially physiotherapy home-exercise
sheets, but almost none are open in a way CC BY-SA 4.0 content can use. The usual
reasons: a non-commercial licence, images carved out of an otherwise open licence,
drawings owned by the commercial exercise software the sheet was made with, or plain
"free to download" wording, which is not a licence. The one route worth a per-file look
is Physiopedia, whose uploads carry their own licences.

# Bodies checked

| Body | Kind | Licence as stated | Images | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| World Health Organization | International | CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO; permission needed for commercial use[^who-copyright] | Third-party material and the WHO logo excluded; some figures, tables, maps and photos need permission[^who-copyright] | **Near miss**: NC conflicts with CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| NHS website (UK) | National health service | OGL v3.0 under the NHS's own terms, which add attribution and adaptation rules[^nhs-terms] | Logos, visuals and image rights need consent; the exclusion list names every strength, balance, flexibility and sitting-exercise image[^nhs-excluded] | **Not used** |
| US health agencies (NIA, CDC, ODPHP) | National public-health agencies | Public domain by statute, except material credited to others[^licences] | Photos by private studios, a guide co-developed with a university, campaign images only partly public domain[^publications][^odphp-copyright] | **Not used** so far; see the publications page |
| Australian and New Zealand health agencies | National public-health agencies | CC BY 4.0 with listed exclusions[^licences] | Images and photographs commonly excluded; New Zealand's injury-prevention warm-ups are built on a sport body's own programme[^acc-sportsmart] | **Not used** |
| Canadian health agencies | National public-health agencies | Crown copyright; non-commercial reproduction terms[^licences] | | **Not used** |
| Physiopedia | Non-profit physiotherapy wiki | Text under a Creative Commons licence; each upload chooses from a menu that includes CC BY-SA 3.0 and 4.0, CC BY, "special permission" and "allow non-commercial"[^physiopedia-licences] | Pages for the plank and bridging use several images; their file pages were not reachable for a licence read[^physiopedia-plank][^physiopedia-bridging] | **Per file only**: a file is a candidate when its own page says CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 3.0 or 4.0 and names an author who made it; "special permission" and non-commercial files are not used |
| Hospital physiotherapy leaflets | Home-exercise sheets | Usually the trust's copyright, with drawings from commercial exercise software whose vendor keeps the media rights[^physio-software] | Commercial leaflet sellers allow sharing with patients but forbid reproduction[^anatomystuff] | **Not used**: "free to share" is not a licence, and the drawings are third-party |

# Rules for this vein

- **NC and ND are out.** A non-commercial or no-derivatives licence cannot flow into CC
  BY-SA 4.0 content, whatever else it allows. Record it as a near miss.
- **"Free" is not a licence.** "Free to download", "free for personal use", "for patient
  education" or "share with your patients" gives no right to adapt and republish.
- **Look past the sheet to the drawing.** Physiotherapy sheets are commonly assembled in
  commercial software; the sheet's publisher does not own the drawings.
- **No logos, no league or programme branding.** Injury-prevention programmes from sport
  governing bodies are their copyright and carry their marks.
- **Same rules as government works:** illustrations only, no photos of people, no doses,
  each figure's own credit read.[^licences]
