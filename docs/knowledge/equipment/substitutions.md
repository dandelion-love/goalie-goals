# Principle

A substitute must train the same quality, at the same or lower risk, with equipment the
household has, and must itself be approved by the owner. A heavier or improvised object
is never a substitute: home equipment and missing supervision are known injury
sources.[^nsca-2009] When no swap meets these tests, the exercise is left out.

# Swap table

| Missing item | Safe substitute | Basis | No safe substitute for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bands` | Harder bodyweight variant (single-leg, slower lowering, longer hold) | Bands and conventional resistance give similar strength gains;[^lopes-2019] bodyweight progressions are an accepted youth mode[^nsca-2009] | Band-resisted shoulder rotations: swap to a bodyweight shoulder exercise (wall slides) |
| `medicine ball` | Throws with a football (`other:ball`) against a wall; plyometric push-ups on knees | Same throwing pattern, lighter load | None needed |
| `Gym mat` | Grass at a park or field | Soft surface for landings | **Dives and dive progressions** when there is neither a mat nor grass: left out |
| `other:cone` | Any flat marker (a sock, tape on a hard floor) | Distances only need marking | None needed |
| `other:step` | The bottom stair | Same height range | Jump-ups when only stairs exist: left out (no jumping onto stairs) |
| `other:wall` | A partner throwing the ball | Same catching practice | Solo rebound drills: left out |
| `other:ball` | None indoors | | Catching and handling drills: left out |
| `Pull-up bar` | Band rows, if the profile has `bands` | Pulling strength with a measured load[^lopes-2019] | All hanging exercises, and all pulling work when there are no bands either: left out |
| Open space of about 20 m (22 yd) | Shorter starts (2 to 10 m, 2 to 11 yd) indoors or in a yard | The sprint dose uses about 20 m efforts;[^moran-2017-sprint] shorter starts still train acceleration | Full-length sprints: left out |
| Soft surface for jumping | Lower-impact variants (pogo hops in place, step-ups) | Jump training is safe only with built-in safety rules[^johnson-2011] | Repeated bounds and tuck jumps on hard floors: left out |

# Never used as substitutes

- Backpacks or bags filled with books or water, furniture, a sibling's body weight, or any
  other improvised load.
- Chairs, tables or beds as jump boxes.
- Concrete or tiled floors for jumps or dives.
- Heavier equipment than the exercise declared.
