# Patterns

* [The lesson path](/ux/lesson-path.md) - A single guided route through the content, shown as a sequence of steps, so the learner never has to choose what to do next.
* [Bite-size sessions](/ux/bite-size-sessions.md) - Sessions short enough to start on a low-motivation day, with a small daily minimum that still counts.
* [Immediate feedback](/ux/immediate-feedback.md) - Every answer or action gets an instant, specific response, framed around the task rather than the person.
* [Celebration moments](/ux/celebration-moments.md) - Short, earned animations at session end and at milestones that mark progress without turning into a slot machine.
* [Character and tone](/ux/character-and-tone.md) - A recurring guide character and a warm, playful voice give the app personality; guilt-tripping personas are the known failure mode.
* [Sound and haptics](/ux/sound-and-haptics.md) - Short sounds and vibrations confirm actions and mark success; they must be sparse, meaningful and easy to turn off.
* [Onboarding](/ux/onboarding.md) - Let people do a first real session before asking for an account, and ask only the questions that shape the plan.
* [Notifications](/ux/notifications.md) - Daily reminders tuned by experiment are a major retention lever, and a major source of pressure on teens; use few, user-timed, kind ones.
* [Visual language](/ux/visual-language.md) - Bold colour, rounded shapes, simple illustrations and large touch targets make the app feel friendly and easy to use at a glance.
