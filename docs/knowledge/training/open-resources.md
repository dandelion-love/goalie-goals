# Principle

Do not reinvent the wheel: the app's training rules restate published position statements
and reviews. It copies no text from them; every rule is a paraphrase with a footnote, so
the sources' copyright terms are respected whatever their licence.

# Resources

| Resource | What the app takes from it | Access and licence |
|---|---|---|
| NSCA youth resistance training position statement, 2009[^nsca-2009] | General guidelines table: supervision, warm-up, sets, repetitions, 5 to 10% progression, frequency | Journal article, copyright NSCA; the NSCA posts a PDF on its site; cited and paraphrased only |
| 2014 International Consensus on youth resistance training[^lloyd-2014] | Qualified supervision, technique first, maximal-testing caveats | Journal article, copyright BMJ; licence not confirmed as open; cited and paraphrased only |
| IOC consensus on youth athletic development, 2015[^ioc-2015] | Growth and injury, rest, sleep, nutrition, pain rule | Published open access in Br J Sports Med (BMJ); cited and paraphrased only |
| WHO physical activity guidelines, 2020[^who-2020] | Weekly activity and strengthening baseline | WHO publication under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO; cited and paraphrased only |
| Concussion in Sport Group consensus, 2023[^concussion-2023] | Remove from play and see a professional on any suspected concussion | Journal article, copyright BMJ; the app links to the group's recognition tools rather than copying them, so their reuse terms do not need to be settled |
| AAP, Sports Dietitians Australia, Canadian Paediatric Society, Health Canada, EFSA, AASM and national food guides | Paediatric, nutrition and sleep guidance | Cited and paraphrased only; see each concept's sources |

# What the app does not reuse

No exercise images, videos or programme templates from these sources are copied into the
repo. Exercise data and equipment names come from open datasets chosen in the equipment
area (see [equipment model](/decisions/equipment-model.md)).
