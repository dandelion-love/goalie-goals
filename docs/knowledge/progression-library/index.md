# Progression library

The movement patterns a young keeper trains, a ladder of three to five rungs for each from
basic to harder with household equipment only, and the rules for when a rung unlocks and
when it steps back down.

# Patterns

* [Movement patterns for a young keeper's progression library](/progression-library/movement-patterns.md) - The sixteen ladders the library covers (strength, trunk, movement and jumping, and goalkeeper actions), each tied to a goalkeeper need, with what the default household profile can run.

# Ladders

* [Strength ladders - squat, hinge, single-leg, push and pull](/progression-library/ladders-strength.md) - Four-rung ladders for the five strength patterns with household equipment only, each rung with its starting dose inside the training rules, its source, the cues a parent can check, its unlock dose and where it steps down to.
* [Trunk ladders - anti-extension, anti-rotation and lateral flexion](/progression-library/ladders-trunk.md) - Three-rung ladders for the trunk patterns with household equipment only, each rung with its starting dose inside the training rules and their hold limits, its source, the cues a parent can check, its unlock dose and where it steps down to.
* [Movement and jump ladders - lateral movement, jump and land, reactive ankle](/progression-library/ladders-movement-and-jumps.md) - Ladders for moving sideways, jumping and landing, and quick ankle hops with household equipment only, each rung with its starting dose inside the training rules' effort and jump-contact limits, its source, the cues a parent can check, its unlock dose and where it steps down to.
* [Goalkeeper ladders - set position, footwork, collapse and low dive, high catch, punch](/progression-library/ladders-goalkeeper.md) - Ladders for the goalkeeper actions with household equipment only, the dive ladder starting from kneeling on a soft surface, each rung with its starting dose inside the training rules, its source, the cues a parent can check, its unlock dose and where it steps down to.

# Rules

* [Unlock rules for the progression library](/progression-library/unlock-rules.md) - How a rung unlocks and steps back down, refining the training-rules progression rule - what a clean repetition is, the test every parent cue must pass (and the rejection when it fails), steps inside a rung, two clean sessions in a row, the parent's in-app approval as the last step, and the step-down triggers.
