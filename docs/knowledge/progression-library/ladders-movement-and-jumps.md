# How to read the tables

The columns mean what they mean in the [strength ladders](/progression-library/ladders-strength.md).
The limits that apply here:[^training-rules]

- **Jumps and hops** are counted in foot contacts (a two-footed landing is one contact): 1
  to 3 sets of 3 to 6 on a card, a session starting at 50 to 60 contacts and never above
  about 100, at most twice a week, on a forgiving surface, never concrete. Landings are
  taught first and every jump rung here ends in a frozen landing, because landing quality,
  not height, decides whether a jump can progress.[^plyo][^johnson-2011] A landing that breaks is an unclean repetition.[^unlock]
- **Lateral efforts** follow the sprint and change-of-direction limit: efforts of 20 m or
  less with at least 90 s rest; for a keeper, short shuffles over 2 to 4 m.[^speed][^asadi-2017]
- During reported fast growth, jump rungs hold and drop back to their starting
  dose.[^moran-2017-cmj][^training-rules]

| 1 | `side-step` | Step-together side steps at walking pace between two spots 4 m apart, low stance | `body only` | efforts, 2 x 3 of 4 m, rest 90 s | The head stays at the same height (no bobbing up); the feet never cross; hands stay up | Card's `easier`; shuffles for agility[^speed] | 3 x 4 | Shorter distance (2 m) |
| 2 | `lateral-shuffle` *(card)* | Quick low shuffle between two spots | `body only` | efforts, 2 x 3 of 4 m, rest 90 s | The head stays level; the feet never cross; hands stay up and ready | Short shuffles for keepers[^speed][^asadi-2017] | 3 x 4 | `side-step` |
| 3 | `shuffle-change` | Shuffle on the parent's point and change direction once when the parent points the other way | `body only` | efforts, 2 x 3 of 4 m, rest 90 s | Changes direction without standing up (head height stays the same); the feet never cross; no slip on the turn | Change of direction with full recovery[^speed][^asadi-2017] | 3 x 4 | `lateral-shuffle` |

# Jump and land (`jl`)

| # | Rung id | Rung | Equipment | Starting dose | Clean cues (parent side-on) | Source | Unlock at | Step down to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `snap-down` | Rise onto the toes, drop into the landing shape and freeze, no jump | `body only` | contacts, 2 x 5, rest 60 s | Freezes for 2 s; in the freeze, knees over the toes; lands quietly | Landings before jumps[^plyo][^johnson-2011] | 3 x 6 | Slower drop, hands on hips |
| 2 | `broad-jump-stick` *(card)* | Short forward jump, freeze on landing | `body only` | contacts, 3 x 3, rest 90 s | Freezes for 2 s; knees bend and stay over the toes; lands quietly on both feet | Plyometrics in youth[^johnson-2011] | 3 x 5 | `snap-down` |
| 3 | `squat-jump-stick` | Jump straight up at about half effort, land and freeze | `body only` | contacts, 3 x 3, rest 90 s | Freezes for 2 s with knees over the toes; lands within a foot length of the take-off spot; lands quietly | FIFA 11+ vertical jumps;[^soligard-2008] few high-quality reps[^nsca-2009] | 3 x 5 | `broad-jump-stick` |
| 4 | `lateral-jump-stick` | Two-footed jump sideways over a line, land and freeze, then back | `body only` | contacts, 3 x 3 per side, rest 90 s | Freezes for 2 s with knees over the toes; both feet land at the same time (one sound); lands quietly | FIFA 11+ lateral jumps;[^soligard-2008] agility gains from jumps[^asadi-2017] | 3 x 4 per side | `squat-jump-stick` |

Not in the ladder yet: single-leg hops to a stick, which load one leg on every landing; a
later rung on a soft surface only, after the owner decides. Tuck jumps, repeated bounds,
box jumps and drop jumps stay out under the plyometric rules.[^plyo]
