# How to read the tables

The columns mean what they mean in the [strength ladders](/progression-library/ladders-strength.md).
No youth study tests a progression for goalkeeper technique. These ladders apply the
general youth rules (technique before load, simple before complex, few high-quality
repetitions for power) to the goalkeeper actions;[^nsca-2009][^nsca-ltad-2016] they are a
cautious reading, not a tested protocol, and the owner's approval of each rung is the
check on them.[^training-rules]

- **Doses.** Technique rungs with no jump use the repetition dose (1 to 2 sets of 10 to
  15). Dives, jump-catches and punches at speed are power work: 1 to 3 sets of 3 to 6, few
  and high-quality.[^training-rules][^nsca-2009] Every dive and jump-catch landing counts
  as one contact toward the session's jump limit (start 50 to 60, never above about
  100).[^plyo] Footwork uses the effort limit with at least 90 s rest.[^speed]
- **Gloves.** Any catching or punching rung is done in goalkeeper gloves.[^gk-power]
- **Soft surface.** Dives and jump-catches need a mat (`Gym mat`) or grass; with neither,
  the app leaves them out and never substitutes a hard floor.[^equipment-model][^substitutions]
- **A partner.** Catching and punching rungs need the parent to toss the ball; the
  substitutions table already uses a partner in place of a wall.[^substitutions]

# Footwork (`fw`)

| # | Rung id | Rung | Equipment | Starting dose | Clean cues (parent in front) | Source | Unlock at | Step down to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `quick-feet-set` | Quick small steps on the spot for 3 s, then set on the parent's call | `body only` | efforts, 2 x 4, rest 90 s | Feet stay low and inside a small square; ends in a set stance (set-position cues); no stumble | Short, full-quality efforts[^speed] | 3 x 4 | `set-position` |
| 2 | `shuffle-to-set` | Shuffle 2 m to a marker and set facing the parent | `body only` | efforts, 2 x 4 of 2 m, rest 90 s | Arrives with the chest facing the parent; set stance on arrival; the feet never cross | Shuffles into a set position[^speed][^gk-power] | 3 x 4 | `quick-feet-set` |
| 3 | `drop-step-shuffle` | From set, first step back on the diagonal, then shuffle to a marker and set | `body only` | efforts, 2 x 4 of 2 m, rest 90 s | The first foot lands behind a line on the floor (tape or a flat marker); the chest stays toward the parent; ends in a set stance | Short, full-quality efforts[^speed] | 3 x 4 | `shuffle-to-set` |

`drop-step-shuffle` is a worked rejection case. Its first draft cue, "opens the hip and
steps back on a 45 degree line", fails the cue test: the angle needs a trained eye and the
step takes a fraction of a second. It was reworded to an outcome a parent can see, the
foot landing behind a line on the floor.[^unlock]

# Collapse and low dive (`dv`)

Kneeling on a soft surface first, then a crouch, then standing, then with a step: the
sequence the goalkeeper research gives, as a cautious application of technique before
load.[^gk-power] Each rung needs `Gym mat` or `place:grass`, and `other:ball` from rung 2.
Stop at once, and record it, if the keeper's head hits the ground, or the keeper lands on
an elbow or the tip of the shoulder; a knock to the head followed by any symptom ends the
day under the training rules' warning signs.[^gk-power][^training-rules]

| # | Rung id | Rung | Equipment | Starting dose | Clean cues (parent in front, 2 m away) | Source | Unlock at | Step down to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `kneeling-side-fall` | Kneel tall, arms forward; fall sideways onto the side of the body, no ball; roll back up | `body only`; `Gym mat` or grass | contacts, 2 x 3 per side, rest 90 s | No thud on landing; the head never touches the mat; ends lying on the side facing the parent, not on the back or front; the keeper says nothing landed hard | Dive sequence from kneeling[^gk-power] | 3 x 3 per side | Left out (card's `easier`: side-lying roll on the mat) |
| 2 | `kneeling-collapse-to-ball` | As rung 1, falling onto a still ball beside the knee, one hand behind it and one on top | `body only`, `other:ball`; `Gym mat` or grass | contacts, 2 x 3 per side, rest 90 s | The ball is still held in both hands at the end; no thud; the head never touches the mat; ends on the side facing the parent | Dive sequence from kneeling[^gk-power] | 3 x 3 per side | `kneeling-side-fall` |
| 3 | `crouch-collapse` | From a low crouch, collapse onto a still ball placed a short reach to the side | `body only`, `other:ball`; `Gym mat` or grass | contacts, 2 x 3 per side, rest 90 s | As rung 2 | Dive sequence, crouch stage[^gk-power] | 3 x 3 per side | `kneeling-collapse-to-ball` |
| 4 | `standing-collapse` | From the set position, collapse onto a ball the parent rolls slowly a step to the side | `body only`, `other:ball`; `Gym mat` or grass | contacts, 2 x 3 per side, rest 90 s | As rung 2, and the keeper starts from a set stance (set-position cues) | Dive sequence, standing stage[^gk-power] | 3 x 3 per side | `crouch-collapse` |
| 5 | `step-low-dive` | One side step, then a low dive onto a ball rolled about 1 m beyond the step | `body only`, `other:ball`; `Gym mat` or grass | contacts, 2 x 3 per side, rest 90 s | As rung 4; lands with the ball held and the body facing the parent; no bounce off the elbow (the keeper says nothing landed hard) | Dive sequence, with a step[^gk-power] | 3 x 3 per side | `standing-collapse` |

**Rejection case worked here.** The first draft's cue for every dive rung was "lands on
the side of the calf, thigh, hip and then the shoulder, in that order". A parent cannot
judge it: the order of contact takes a fraction of a second and needs coaching knowledge
to read. It was reworded into outcomes the parent can check after the dive (no thud, ball
held, head clear of the mat, finishing position, the keeper's own report). Had no outcome
cue worked, the rung would have been split into a slower version or dropped.[^unlock]

# Punch (`ph`)

Punching speed comes from pushes and throws, and the skill from a ball tossed softly;
never from striking a heavy or hard object.[^gk-power]

| # | Rung id | Rung | Equipment | Starting dose | Clean cues (parent side-on) | Source | Unlock at | Step down to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `wall-chest-pass` | Two-handed chest pass of a football to a wall from 2 m; catch the rebound | `body only`, `other:ball`, `other:wall`, `other:goalkeeper-gloves` | reps, 2 x 10, rest 60 s | The ball leaves from the chest with both hands; both arms finish straight together; the ball hits the wall at about chest height | Throws for upper-body power;[^gk-power] football in place of a medicine ball[^substitutions] | 2 x 15 | Closer to the wall (1 m) |
| 2 | `punch-shape` | From set, bring the fists together at the chest and punch up and forward, no ball; freeze | `body only` | reps, 2 x 10, rest 45 s | In the freeze the two fists touch side by side; the arms are straight together at head height; the feet have not moved | Technique before speed[^nsca-2009] | 2 x 15 | `wall-chest-pass` |
| 3 | `two-fist-punch` | The parent tosses underarm from about 3 m to head height; punch it back up and away, freeze | `body only`, `other:ball` | contacts, 3 x 5, rest 90 s | The ball goes back higher than the parent's head; in the freeze the fists are still together; the arms finish straight | Power as few, high-quality reps;[^nsca-2009] punching with a soft toss[^gk-power] | 3 x 6 | `punch-shape` |
