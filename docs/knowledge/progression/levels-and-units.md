# Mechanic

Content is split into units of a handful of sessions, each closing with a checkpoint or
review. A level or unit number shows how far the learner has come.

# Evidence

- The reference app organises its path into units of mixed new and review sessions,
  explicitly to give more structure and guidance.[^duo-path]
- A medium-term goal close enough to see produces goal-gradient acceleration; goals that
  feel too far away do not.[^kivetz]
- Perceived competence, the sense of growing mastery, is one of the strongest predictors
  of enjoyment and continued play in games.[^ryan-games] Levels make competence visible.

# Evidence gaps (AC4)

No published study isolates levels or units as a habit-builder; their value is mostly
structural (orientation) and motivational (competence), not habit-forming in itself.

# Takeaways

Units map naturally onto training blocks (a few weeks of one emphasis). A unit
"checkpoint" is a form check, not a max test. See
[progression design](/decisions/progression-design.md).
