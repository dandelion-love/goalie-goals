# Science

- Three basic needs, autonomy (choosing), competence (getting better) and relatedness
  (connection), underpin intrinsic motivation and wellbeing; settings that support them
  produce more durable motivation.[^sdt]
- Games pull people in largely because they satisfy those needs; perceived autonomy and
  competence predicted enjoyment and continued play.[^ryan-games]
- Across 128 experiments, expected tangible rewards tied to engaging in, completing or
  performing a task reduced later free-choice interest; positive, informational feedback
  did not.[^dkr]
- Reward-heavy classroom gamification lowered intrinsic motivation over a
  semester.[^hanus]

# Mapping to the mechanics

| Need | Supports it | Undermines it |
|---|---|---|
| Autonomy | Choosing training days, daily target, focus area; opting out of any mechanic | Guilt messages, forced daily use, paywalls |
| Competence | Form feedback, technique milestones, unit checkpoints | Volume-based points, ranking against strangers |
| Relatedness | Doing it together, shared progress, mutual thanks | Competition between the two people |
