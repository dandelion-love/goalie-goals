# Mechanics

* [Streaks and streak protection](/progression/streaks.md) - A count of consecutive active days motivates through visible progress and then loss aversion; protection items soften breaks, and broken streaks demotivate.
* [Points and daily goals](/progression/xp-and-daily-goals.md) - Points for each activity plus a user-chosen daily target turn effort into a visible, closable goal; points reward volume unless designed otherwise.
* [Levels and units](/progression/levels-and-units.md) - Grouping content into units with checkpoints, and showing a level, gives medium-term goals and a sense of competence.
* [Leagues and leaderboards](/progression/leagues-and-leaderboards.md) - Weekly ranked tables of similar users with promotion and demotion raise time spent, but evidence in education is mixed and can lower motivation.
* [Quests](/progression/quests.md) - Small daily, monthly and shared challenges give variety and short goals; shared quests add cooperation, but evidence on habit is thin.
* [Badges and achievements](/progression/badges.md) - Collectible markers for milestones give recognition and goals; informational badges help, controlling or volume badges can backfire.
* [Variable rewards](/progression/variable-rewards.md) - Unpredictable rewards (chests, random bonuses) sustain engagement through the same mechanism as gambling; no evidence they build a learning habit, and evidence of harm for young people when tied to spending.
* [Reminders](/progression/reminders.md) - Daily reminders act as the prompt in the habit loop and measurably bring people back, but optimised reminder copy drifts toward pressure, and teens are already overloaded.

# Behavioural science

* [Habit loops](/progression/habit-loops.md) - Habits form when a behaviour is repeated in a stable context until the cue triggers it automatically; it takes weeks to months and tolerates the odd miss.
* [Goal-gradient and endowed progress](/progression/goal-gradient.md) - People work harder as a visible goal gets closer, and a head start on progress increases completion; both can be used kindly or to drive volume.
* [Loss aversion](/progression/loss-aversion.md) - Losses weigh more than equal gains; streak systems rely on it once a streak is long, which is also what makes them stressful.
* [Self-determination theory](/progression/self-determination-theory.md) - Lasting motivation rests on autonomy, competence and relatedness; rewards that feel controlling erode it, rewards that inform support it.
