# Exercise dossiers

What an exercise dossier holds, and the rules for what it links to.

## Clip links

* [Policy for linking goalkeeper clips from a dossier](clip-link-policy.md) - Official channels only, opened outside the app with no embed, tracking or referrer, within the platform's age rules, chosen by the move with a spread across clubs, leagues and countries, and approved by the parent once for the household and per link with its exercise.
* [Clip link record and the pre-release link check](clip-link-record-and-check.md) - The fields each clip link records, and the check that strips tracking and flags dead, private, redirected, age-restricted or re-owned links and an uneven spread.
* [Sample clip links: the low diving save](clip-sample-low-diving-save.md) - Three official-channel clips of adult professionals making a low dive, across three confederations and both games, with the candidates turned down.
