# Exercise dossiers

What an exercise dossier holds, the rules every claim in it follows, the evidence behind "what to expect", the rules for linking clips, and a worked example.

# Content and claims

* [The sections of an exercise dossier](/dossiers/sections.md) - The six sections an exercise dossier shows, in order, and what each must and must not hold.
* [Claim rules for exercise dossiers](/dossiers/claim-rules.md) - Youth sources first, adult-only evidence labelled, outcomes as ranges with time frames, no promises about looks, size, shape or weight, injury claims no stronger than the evidence, and plain words a teen reads.
* [What training changes in a young athlete, and how fast](/dossiers/youth-adaptations.md) - Youth strength gains are mostly neural, come as ranges over weeks to months, vary with maturation, and fade unevenly when training stops.

# Clip links

* [Policy for linking goalkeeper clips from a dossier](/dossiers/clip-link-policy.md) - Official channels only, opened outside the app with no embed, tracking or referrer, within the platform's age rules, chosen by the move with a spread across clubs, leagues and countries, and approved by the parent once for the household and per link with its exercise.
* [Clip link record and the pre-release link check](/dossiers/clip-link-record-and-check.md) - The fields each clip link records, and the check that strips tracking and flags dead, private, redirected, age-restricted or re-owned links and an uneven spread.
* [Sample clip links: the low diving save](/dossiers/clip-sample-low-diving-save.md) - Three official-channel clips of adult professionals making a low dive, across three confederations and both games, with the candidates turned down.
