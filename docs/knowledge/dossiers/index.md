# Exercise dossiers

What an exercise dossier holds, the rules every claim in it follows, the evidence behind "what to expect", and a worked example.

* [The sections of an exercise dossier](/dossiers/sections.md) - The six sections an exercise dossier shows, in order, and what each must and must not hold.
* [Claim rules for exercise dossiers](/dossiers/claim-rules.md) - Youth sources first, adult-only evidence labelled, outcomes as ranges with time frames, no promises about looks, size, shape or weight, injury claims no stronger than the evidence, and plain words a teen reads.
* [What training changes in a young athlete, and how fast](/dossiers/youth-adaptations.md) - Youth strength gains are mostly neural, come as ranges over weeks to months, vary with maturation, and fade unevenly when training stops.
