# 1. Why it matters for a goalkeeper

> **Headline:** A strong, steady middle keeps your body in one piece when you dive, jump
> and land.

> **Dives.** When you dive, your trunk links the push from your legs to your hands
> reaching for the ball. In a study of academy keepers, dives to the weaker side showed
> less trunk control and less explosive push.[^dipaolo-2022]

> **Set position.** In the ready stance you hold still, then push off fast any way.
> Coaches of young players train the middle because it steadies the spine and hips and
> passes force between legs and arms.[^fifa-core-2025] In studies with adults, a stiffer
> middle lets the push from the hips reach the arms and legs.[^mcgill-2010]

> **Jumps and landings.** Keepers make only a few saves a match, but they are short,
> explosive dives and jumps.[^white-2018] A middle that does not fold helps you land in
> control.[^fifa-core-2025]

# 2. What to expect if you keep it up

> **In a few weeks:** holding the plank feels steadier, and your hips stop sagging before
> the time is up. The app adds at most 5 seconds at a time, after two clean sessions and
> your parent's OK, up to 30 seconds; after that the next step is the harder version, not
> a longer hold.[^training-rules][^card]

> **In 6 weeks:** in a trial with school pupils in their early teens, training the trunk
> twice a week improved trunk strength tests by 8 to 22%, a sideways jumping test by 4 to
> 5%, and a balance test by 2 to 3%.[^granacher-2014]

> **Over 9 weeks:** in older elite youth players who added trunk training 2 to 3 times a
> week to football, back strength improved by about 5% and short sprints by about
> 3%.[^prieske-2016]

> **Why it works so fast:** at your age most of the early gain is your brain and nerves
> learning to switch the muscles on together, not the muscles getting
> bigger.[^csep-2008][^ozmun-1994]

> **Keep it up:** about twice a week, as in your plan, keeps the gains coming. In one
> youth study, jump gains from 12 weeks of training held for 8 weeks after it
> stopped,[^nunes-2021] but nobody has tested how long plank gains last. Everyone grows
> and improves at a different pace, so compare yourself only with yourself.[^ioc-2015]

# 5. How to do it

The front plank card, unchanged: forearms under shoulders, a straight line from head to
heels, steady breathing, two holds of 20 seconds with 45 seconds' rest, and its stop
signs.[^card]

# 6. The science

> **Mechanism.** The plank is a hold: the trunk muscles work together to stop your hips
> sagging or piking. In studies with adults, a front plank on the floor works the tummy
> muscles at a moderate level, less than half of their hardest effort, which suits
> control and endurance more than all-out strength.[^ekstrom-2007]

> **Harder versions.** In studies with adults, planks on wobbly equipment made the tummy
> and back muscles work harder.[^snarr-2014] The app makes the plank harder by lifting a
> foot, one step at a time.[^card][^training-rules]

> **Strength without size.** Studies in young people find strength rises far more than
> muscle size, which points to the nervous system learning, not growth.[^csep-2008][^ozmun-1994]

> **Limits.** No study has tested the plank on its own in goalkeepers, or shown it makes
> saves better. The teen trials above trained several trunk exercises, not only the
> plank, and had no group that did no training.[^granacher-2014][^prieske-2016]

> **Limits: injuries.** Planks are part of the FIFA 11+ warm-up. In a season-long trial
> with teenage players, the whole warm-up was linked to fewer injuries overall, but no
> trial shows the plank prevents injury on its own.[^soligard-2008]

# Gaps this example found

- No study measured trunk muscle activity in planks in early-teens athletes; the map rests
  on adult data and says so.
- No study links a trunk exercise to goalkeeper saves; the "why" section rests on the
  demands of the position, coaching guidance and one observational dive study.
- The 9-week youth football trial used older elite players, outside the keeper's band;
  it is kept as a second range and named as such.
