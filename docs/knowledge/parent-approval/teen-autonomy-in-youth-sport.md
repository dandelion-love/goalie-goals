# Findings

- A 2024 systematic review found parental autonomy support linked to more self-determined
  motivation in young athletes, in some studies more strongly than coach
  support.[^gao-2024]
- The same review found perceived parental pressure linked to amotivation, less enjoyment
  and lower need satisfaction, and noted negative parent behaviour seems to predict more
  strongly than positive.[^gao-2024]
- Autonomy-supportive adults offer choices, explain the reason for a task and keep
  controlling language down; this helps young people internalise goals.[^hui-2012]
- In youth sport, autonomy-supportive coaching was associated with need satisfaction and
  with life skills, through that need satisfaction.[^cronin-2022]
- Regimes place the start of a young person's own consent differently: under 13 needs a
  parent in the US,[^coppa-faq] the EU default is 16 with member states free to go down to
  13,[^gdpr-art8] and Canadian guidance ties it to maturity above 13.[^opc-consent] An
  early-teens athlete sits in the band where they disagree, so the app treats the parent's
  consent as needed and the keeper's agreement as wanted.
- Where parents can monitor a child, the child should get age-appropriate information
  about it and a clear sign that it is active; this is part of the common baseline across
  the regimes surveyed.[^ico-std11][^baseline]

# What this means for the design

| Keeper decides alone | Parent approves | Both see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| order of approved sessions, which approved alternative drill, doing less, rest days, effort ratings, notes, look and sounds, asking for something new | anything that adds risk or load, or shares data | every request, decision and reason, in one history |

- Every request shown to the parent carries the app's reason, so the parent's answer and
  the keeper's view both rest on a rationale, not a bare yes or no.[^hui-2012]
- The keeper can propose; a decline comes with a reason and a path to ask again, which
  keeps the approval from feeling like pressure.[^gao-2024]
- The keeper can always see what the parent can see.[^ico-std11][^baseline]
