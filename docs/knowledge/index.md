# goalie-goals knowledge

Research findings and decisions behind the app, as an Open Knowledge Format bundle.

## Research

* [Equipment at home](/equipment/index.md) - bodyweight minimum, home and outdoor equipment, substitutions and an open equipment vocabulary

* [Safe training for U15 athletes](/training/index.md) - youth resistance training, plyometrics, speed, goalkeeper power, recovery, sleep and nutrition

* [Progression and habit](/progression/index.md) - streaks, XP, levels, quests, badges, rewards and reminders, the science behind them, and the dark patterns refused

* [UX patterns](/ux/index.md) - the lesson path, short sessions, feedback, celebration, tone, sound, onboarding, notifications and visual language

* [Parent approval](/parent-approval/index.md) - how a parent approves what the app asks of a teen, and the privacy rules for minors

* [Building for the iPad](/platform/index.md) - installing, layout, touch, push, offline, media and sign-in for an iPad home-screen web app
