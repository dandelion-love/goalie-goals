# goalie-goals knowledge

Research findings and decisions behind the app, as an Open Knowledge Format bundle.

## Research

* [Exercise media](/exercise-media/index.md) - open exercise media, illustration sets, animation, drawn figures and the stored format

* [CC0 media](/cc0-media/index.md) - public-domain exercise figures and pose references with the best figure per exercise, reward and path art (streaks, stars, badges, trophies, path nodes, backgrounds, an original coach), sounds for cues and celebrations, and rigged 3D characters as offline pose references, each licence checked at its source, with shortlists and a recommended style

* [How training apps explain exercises](/exercise-apps/index.md) - open-source and commercial training apps, how they explain exercises, and their licences

* [Equipment at home](/equipment/index.md) - bodyweight minimum, home and outdoor equipment, substitutions and an open equipment vocabulary

* [Safe training for U15 athletes](/training/index.md) - youth resistance training, plyometrics, speed, goalkeeper power, recovery, sleep and nutrition

* [Progression and habit](/progression/index.md) - streaks, XP, levels, quests, badges, rewards and reminders, the science behind them, and the dark patterns refused

* [Motion and celebration](/motion/index.md) - open motion libraries and runtimes, animation file licences, and the motion principles for sets, XP, streaks and the finish

* [UX patterns](/ux/index.md) - the lesson path, short sessions, feedback, celebration, tone, sound, onboarding, notifications and visual language

* [Parent approval](/parent-approval/index.md) - how a parent approves what the app asks of a teen, and the privacy rules for minors

* [Visual identity](/visual-identity/index.md) - open rounded fonts, legibility at a distance, self-hosting, token tooling, the palette method with its contrast table, and licences

* [Building for the iPad](/platform/index.md) - installing, layout, touch, push, offline, media and sign-in for an iPad home-screen web app

* [Quality measures](/quality/index.md) - usability heuristics, SUS, WCAG 2.2, platform guidelines, legibility at a distance, visual consistency, game feel, motion, sound, copy and response times, and what each means for a two-person training app
* [Usability](/usability/index.md) - lightweight usability methods for one household, and the privacy-safe ten-minute home check with its proxy check
