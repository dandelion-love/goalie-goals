# Candidate dossier: paused box squat

Status: **draft for source and content review**. This is not a published dossier, an approval, or an instruction to train. The exact two-second pause above a box is a proposed movement from the existing progression library. No source below establishes that this exact version improves the keeper's performance or is appropriate for an individual.

## What the card asks

Use a stable low box or step. Lower with control, pause for two seconds above the box without sitting, then stand. The proposed starting dose is two sets of ten with 60 seconds of rest. The card's full candidate text and stop signs are in `squat-to-box-pause.json`. This description is a proposal from the project's ladder, not an independent claim of benefit.

## What the research can and cannot say

- A 1993 study compared squat movements with and without a pause in children and adults, including unloaded conditions. It measured oxygen use and efficiency during those movements. It did not test this exact pause above a box, goalkeeper performance, a training program, or an individual's outcome. [Villagra, Cooke and McDonagh (1993)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8149936/).
- A 2010 comparison measured muscle activity and force in eight resistance-trained adults during loaded squats and box squats. It was not a study of children or the unloaded pause above a box in this candidate. Its findings cannot establish this card's effects for the keeper. [McBride and colleagues (2010)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21132859/).
- The existing squat dossier cites adult squat muscle measurements and youth training studies. Those studies do not by themselves establish an extra benefit from this particular pause. The paused variation's primary and secondary muscle map and comparative training effect remain **unavailable** until specifically reviewed and approved.

## Approval and safety

The exact card, neutral fallback figure, dossier and sources need independent review and owner content approval. A parent must separately approve the exact version in the app before any real use. The prior dose must reach its approved target through reviewed single-lever steps, with two clean sessions and parent approval for each change. The current starting card is two sets of ten; the final 14-to-15 step needs rule clarification. Pain, growth, breaks, restart and step-down conditions remain separate holds.
