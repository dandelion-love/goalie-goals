# Acceptance spec: Wall chest pass (`wall-chest-pass`)

The [goalkeeper ladders](/progression-library/ladders-goalkeeper.md) name this as punch rung 1. Wear goalkeeper gloves and use a football, not a medicine ball. Face a solid wall from about 2 m, with nothing breakable nearby and a clear rebound path. Push the ball from the chest with both hands, straighten both arms together, and catch the rebound. The ball hits the wall at about chest height. The parent watches side-on outside the rebound path.

## Dose

The [training rules](/decisions/training-rules.md) set the starting repetition dose.

- kind: reps
- sets: 2
- reps: 10
- per_side: no
- rest_s: 60

## Progression limit

Build toward 2 x 15 clean reps in two sessions. The owner approves a harder rung before use. Step down by moving to 1 m from the wall.

## Stop signs

- Pain in a wrist or finger
- Pain in the shoulder or elbow
- Pain that changes how you move
- Chest pain, a racing heartbeat or unusual shortness of breath
- Dizziness or feeling faint
- Slipping on the floor

## Place in the week

The draft proposes Quick feet, strong arms in place of the wall push-up, preserving the theme and dose. The card must not be offered until the required equipment is available and the owner approves the card and changed session.
