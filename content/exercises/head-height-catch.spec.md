# Acceptance spec: Head-height catch (`head-height-catch`)

The card follows rung 1 of the [goalkeeper ladders](/progression-library/ladders-goalkeeper.md): a parent gently tosses a ball from about 3 m to head height. The keeper catches in front of the face, hands behind the ball and thumbs close, then freezes. Wear goalkeeper gloves. Use a soft ball indoors and keep the space clear of breakables. The parent waits until the keeper is set before each toss.

## Dose

- kind: reps
- sets: 2
- reps: 10
- per_side: no
- rest_s: 60

## Progression limit

Two sets of 10 catches, resting 60 seconds between sets, use no added load. Build to 2 x 15 clean catches in two sessions; the owner approves a harder rung before use. Step down to a chest-height catch. The parent checks the ball stays in front of the face, does not drop or bobble, and the thumbs are behind the ball in the freeze. Stop if the ball hits the face or a stop sign appears.

## Stop signs

- A ball hits the face
- Pain in a hand, wrist or finger
- Pain that changes how you move
- Chest pain, a racing heartbeat or unusual shortness of breath
- Dizziness or feeling faint
- Slipping on the floor

## Place in the week

The card joins Quick feet, strong arms, in place of lateral shuffle. The set position prepares the keeper to catch; the catch comes before the wall chest pass. The replaced card remains in the library. The plan check enforces the current 15-minute limit.

## Approval

The parent receives a new-exercise request and a revised session request. The keeper cannot play the new card until the parent approves both and confirms gloves. An earlier approved session is a fallback only when its own equipment needs are met.
