# Review candidate: Hip hinge wall tap

This is a locked content candidate, outside the live exercise library. It does not
authorize a session, unlock a rung, or approve a dose. An exact content review and the
separate in-app parent decision are required before use.

## Technique and illustration

The documented hinge ladder proposes standing about one foot length from a fixed wall,
facing away; move the hips back to touch the wall, then stand tall. The parent watches
from the side: bottom touches the wall, shins remain nearly upright, and the back remains
flat rather than curved. The original figure is a simplified position guide, not a video
or a substitute for the written cues. Check its posture and accessibility before use.

## Dose

The documented ladder proposes two sets of ten repetitions, with 60 seconds' rest and
no added load, within the [training rules](/decisions/training-rules.md).

- kind: reps
- sets: 2
- reps: 10
- per_side: no
- rest_s: 60

## Stop signs

- Pain in the lower back, hips or knees
- Pain that changes how you move
- Chest pain, a racing heartbeat or unusual shortness of breath
- Dizziness or feeling faint

bridge card names the single-leg bridge as its harder variant. The bridge starts at
2 × 12 and its listed unlock threshold is 2 × 15. The general two-repetition step
reaches 2 × 14; the documented one-repetition final step explicitly applies to squat.
Neither the sequence nor a new hinge increment is decided by this packet. No promotion
or scheduling may rely on it until the conflict is resolved and all applicable
