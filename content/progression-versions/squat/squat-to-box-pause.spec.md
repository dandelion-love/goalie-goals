# Acceptance spec: Paused box squat (`squat-to-box-pause`)

Status: unapproved candidate. The owner must approve this exact card, spec, figure and
dossier before real use. The parent separately approves the version in the app.

## Technique

Stand in front of a stable box or step. Reach forward, move the hips back, and lower
slowly. Stop just above the box and hold still for two seconds without bouncing. Push
through the whole foot to stand. The parent checks knees over toes, heels down and a
still pause without bouncing. Return to the approved squat to box if the pause or squat
is not controlled.

## Dose

The proposed starting dose is inside the limits in [training rules](/decisions/training-rules.md).

- kind: reps
- sets: 2
- reps: 10
- per_side: no
- rest_s: 60

Tempo: down slow for three, pause two seconds, stand up smoothly. No added load.

## Progression limit

The next variation is squat without the box. Do not move on until the existing ladder's
requirements, two clean sessions and all applicable owner and parent approvals are met.
Pain, growth, a break and step-down rules continue to hold progression as appropriate.

## Stop signs

- Pain in the knee, hip or lower back
- Pain that changes how you move
- Chest pain, a racing heartbeat or unusual shortness of breath
- Dizziness or feeling faint
- Knees keep caving in after a reminder (end the set)

## Acceptance

- The card's dose and stop signs match this spec exactly.
- The figure is the separately reviewed neutral fallback; it illustrates no technique.
- No comparative benefit or muscle map is inferred without reviewed evidence.
